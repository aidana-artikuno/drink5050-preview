What “associated with” means

We stay with the language on the pack. These blends are associated with focus, energy and calm. They are plant-based, naturally caffeinated, made without fillers. They are not a medical claim.

How to brew

Scoop 1–2 tsp into a 6–8 oz cup. Pour hot water. Stir until dissolved. Enjoy hot, or pour over ice. The same four steps for coffee and for matcha.
