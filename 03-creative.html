The story

Two rituals that already held the day.

Hi there,

Dr. Kesha Ajvalia, MD, built her days around two quiet marks: coffee first, matcha later. Medicine, family, a full life. The drinks were never the point — the pause was.

Dr. Shri Ajvalia, PhD, brought the same standard to ingredients. When they learned about the traditionally associated benefits of functional mushrooms, they wanted those rituals to hold more — without asking Kesha to eat a mushroom she does not love.

So they became uncompromising about every cup: real Lion’s Mane, Cordyceps and Reishi — not flavoring. Ceremonial-style matcha. Specialty Arabica with notes of chocolate and blueberry. Taste above all else. Crafted with care in Southern California. Proudly served in select wellness resorts and med spas.
