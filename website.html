Matcha with Mushrooms

Ceremonial-grade matcha from Shizuoka, Japan, with Lion’s Mane, Cordyceps and Reishi. Associated with focus, energy and calm. Ingredients on pack: matcha green tea powder, lion’s mane mushroom powder, cordyceps mushroom powder, reishi mushroom powder. 4 calories a serving. Net wt 1.7 oz (50 g).

Coffee with Mushrooms

Highest-quality Arabica instant coffee from the highlands of Colombia, with the same three mushroom powders. Notes of chocolate and blueberry. Ingredients on pack: instant coffee, lion’s mane, cordyceps, reishi. 1 calorie a serving. Net wt 3 oz (85 g).

Lion’s Mane · Cordyceps · Reishi

Real mushroom powders. Traditionally associated with focus (Lion’s Mane), energy (Cordyceps) and calm (Reishi). We keep pack language: associated with — not a medical claim. Insert supplier and ratio details later if the client wishes to publish them.

How do I prepare it?

Scoop 1–2 tsp into a 6–8 oz cup. Pour hot water. Stir until dissolved. Enjoy hot, or pour over ice. No special tools required — though a bamboo whisk makes the matcha especially fine, as with any ceremonial grade.

How is this different from ordinary matcha or coffee?

With matcha you drink the whole leaf, not only the infusion — more caffeine, more of the leaf’s character, a slower curve when L-theanine is present. The coffee is a specialty Arabica chosen for chocolate and blueberry notes. Both carry the same mushroom powders, so the two rituals of the day stay in one standard.
